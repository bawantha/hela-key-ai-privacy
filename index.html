Hela Key AI

Privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026 · Android package com.bawwasoft.helakeyai

This policy explains how Hela Key AI, provided by BawwaSoft, handles information when you use its Sinhala and English keyboard and optional voice typing. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to bawa.inbox@gmail.com.

Typing and information on your device

The keyboard processes the text you type to enter it into the app you are using. Ordinary keyboard input, clipboard content, and learned words are not sent to our voice service. Keyboard preferences and learned words are stored locally on your device. You can remove learned words using Clear learned words in the app.

When you open the clipboard panel, the keyboard reads your current Android clipboard so you can paste it. Hela Key AI does not maintain a server-side clipboard history. Text you insert into another app is then handled under that app's privacy practices.

Optional microphone and voice typing

Voice typing requires you to enable AI voice input, grant Android microphone permission, and start recording with the microphone button. During a voice session, audio is sent over an encrypted connection to Microsoft Azure Speech to convert it to Sinhala text. The transcript is returned to your device and inserted into the selected text field. Voice typing requires an internet connection.

Our application code does not save recordings or transcripts to a server database or write them into application logs. Microsoft states that its real-time speech-to-text service processes audio in server memory without storing it at rest. See Microsoft's speech-to-text privacy documentation.

If optional transcript correction is enabled by the voice service, dictated text is sent through our backend to Microsoft Azure OpenAI for punctuation and transcription corrections. This uses dictated text, not your ordinary keyboard input or clipboard. The service returns the original transcript when correction is unavailable.

App verification and service operation

Google Play Integrity is used to help prevent unauthorized use of the voice service. Google processes app and device signals, and our backend receives verification results such as app authenticity, installation licensing, device integrity, and a request timestamp. These checks are used for security and access control, not advertising. See Google's privacy policy.

Network services necessarily process connection information such as IP addresses and request timing. Microsoft Azure and Google may process operational and security information according to their service policies. Our backend keeps temporary request counters in memory for rate limiting. The app does not include advertising or third-party marketing analytics, and we do not sell keyboard or voice data.

Service providers and international processing

Microsoft Azure hosts our voice backend and provides speech processing and, when enabled, transcript correction. Google provides Play distribution and integrity verification. These providers may process information outside your country. Their processing is governed by the applicable service terms and privacy practices, including the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Retention and your choices

  • Local settings and learned words remain until you clear them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Android's system clipboard is managed separately by Android.
  • Audio and transcripts are processed for the voice session; our application does not keep a persistent server-side recording or transcript history.
  • You can stop recording, disable AI voice input, or revoke microphone permission in Android Settings. Normal keyboard typing remains available without voice input.
  • If you contact support, your email address and message are used to respond and resolve your request. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations. Do not send passwords or sensitive dictated content in support messages.

Hela Key AI does not require an app account. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have provided to us. We cannot delete text you have already inserted into other apps.

Adults only

Hela Key AI is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the effective date. Material changes to voice-data handling will be disclosed before the changed processing is introduced.
