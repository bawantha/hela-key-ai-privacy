Hela Key AI: data deletion

Hela Key AI is provided by BawwaSoft and does not require an account.

Request deletion

Email bawa.inbox@gmail.com with the subject Hela Key AI data deletion. Tell us which support correspondence or information you previously provided you want deleted. Do not include passwords, recordings, or sensitive dictated text.

We will review your request and delete the information we control that can be associated with it. We may retain information where legally required; if this affects your request, we will explain the reason and applicable retention period. We do not keep a server-side keyboard, clipboard, recording, or transcript history in our application database.

Delete information on your device

Use Clear learned words in the app to remove learned words. Clear Hela Key AI app storage in Android Settings or uninstall it to remove local preferences and learned words. Manage your Android clipboard separately. We cannot delete text you inserted into another app; use that app’s deletion controls.

Microsoft Azure and Google process service and security information under their policies. See our privacy policy for processing details and provider links.
